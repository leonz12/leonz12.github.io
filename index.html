leonz — personal projects

Private tools, built and run by one person for their own use.

This page exists for a small collection of personal software projects. They are hobby tools written by their author, for the author, and run on the author's own equipment.

Not for public use

None of these projects is a product or a service. They are not offered to anyone else, there is nothing to sign up for, and they are not intended for public consumption. They have a single user, who is also their author and operator.

Access to the author's own accounts

Some of these tools sign in to the author's own online accounts — for example the author's own cloud storage — so that they can organise the author's own files automatically. They are only ever authorised against the account of the person running them, and they access no other person's data.
