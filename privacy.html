Privacy Policy

leonz — personal projects · last updated 13 September 2026

These are private, personal projects with a single user, who is also their author and operator. They are not offered to the public and have no other users.

No personal information is collected

These projects do not collect, request, store or process personal information belonging to any other person. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no visitors' data, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is collected from anyone who happens to read this page.

Nothing is accessed without authorisation

Where a tool connects to an online account, it does so only against the author's own account, and only after the author has explicitly granted it permission. It cannot access any account that has not authorised it, and it is never used on anyone else's data.

Nothing is sold or shared

No information is sold, rented, published, or shared with anyone for their own purposes. No information belonging to any other person is transmitted anywhere.

Withdrawing permission

Any permission granted to these tools can be withdrawn at any time by the person who granted it, from their own account's security or permissions settings. Withdrawing permission immediately ends all access.
